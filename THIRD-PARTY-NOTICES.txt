Rocksmith Audio Bridge: third-party notices
===========================================

Rocksmith Audio Bridge is Copyright (c) 2026 Cheesewizard and licensed under the MIT License (LICENSE.txt), for the
code Cheesewizard wrote. NOTICE.txt explains its relation to upstream RSMods. This file lists the third-party
components that ship in a Rocksmith Audio Bridge release, which file each one is in, its licence, and where the
licence text is. Paths under ThirdParty\ are relative to this file (the installer puts it in
<Rocksmith 2014>\RocksmithAudioBridge\).

What ships where
----------------
  xinput1_3.dll            upstream RSMods (External/RSMods, published without a licence; its code remains the
                           property of its authors) built with our mods compiled in
  RocksmithAudioBridgeAsio.dll, RocksmithAudioBridgeAsio64.dll, RocksmithAudioBridgeDriverHost.exe
                           our ASIO bridge driver and its 64-bit host (MIT, ours)
  RocksmithAudioBridgeVstHost.exe
                           the VST host, built with JUCE and therefore AGPLv3 (see JUCE below)
  stompbox-capi.dll        the Stompbox engine, GPL-3.0, a separate DLL
  RSMods\RSMods.exe and its folder
                           upstream RSMods' configurator, installed unchanged from upstream's payload
  RSMods\AudioBridge\      our helper exe (MIT, ours) with ONNX Runtime, soxr.dll and rswindowcapture.dll
  RocksmithAudioBridge\    our desktop app (MIT, ours), self-contained .NET 8 with Avalonia
  UsbDk                    installed system-wide from the official UsbDk 1.0.22 x64 MSI (Apache-2.0)
